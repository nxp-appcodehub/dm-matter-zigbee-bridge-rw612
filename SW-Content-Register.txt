Description:            The application example in this repository enables bridging of non Matter devices such as Legacy Zigbee devices to the Matter ecosystem 
                        using NXP Wireless MCU system composed of NXP RW612. There are two versions of the application on separate branches:
                        - one with onboard OpenThread Border Router support connected to separate NXP K32W0x1 802.15.4 radio transceiver acting as Zigbee coordinator
                        - one with onboard Matter over Wi-Fi support with Zigbee running on the onboard 802.15.4 radio transceiver
